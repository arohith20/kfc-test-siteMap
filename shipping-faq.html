Shipping FAQ

What shipping options are available?

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Otherwise it costs $5.99. Express shipping costs $14.99.

Can I track my order?

Yes. A tracking number is emailed once your order ships. You can also view tracking under Orders in your account.
